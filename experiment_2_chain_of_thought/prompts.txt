========================================
EXPERIMENT 2: CHAIN-OF-THOUGHT PROMPTING
========================================

TASK:
Compare a direct prompt with a prompt that asks the model
to think step by step when solving a multi-step math problem.


========================================
TEST 1: DIRECT PROMPT
========================================

Prompt:

Solve the following problem and give the final answer.

A shop sells notebooks for ₹40 each and pens for ₹15 each.
Maneesh buys 5 notebooks and 4 pens. He pays with ₹500.

How much change should he receive?


========================================
TEST 2: CHAIN-OF-THOUGHT PROMPT
========================================

Prompt:

Solve the following problem. Think step by step and then give
the final answer.

A shop sells notebooks for ₹40 each and pens for ₹15 each.
Maneesh buys 5 notebooks and 4 pens. He pays with ₹500.

How much change should he receive?
