========================================
EXPERIMENT 2: CHAIN-OF-THOUGHT PROMPTING
========================================


TASK:
Compare a direct prompt with a prompt that asks the model
to think step by step when solving a multi-step math problem.


========================================
TEST 1: DIRECT PROMPT
========================================

Prompt:

Solve the following problem and give the final answer.

A shop sells notebooks for ₹40 each and pens for ₹15 each.
Maneesh buys 5 notebooks and 4 pens. He pays with ₹500.

How much change should he receive?


RAW GEMINI OUTPUT:

The total cost of the notebooks is 5 × ₹40 = ₹200.
The total cost of the pens is 4 × ₹15 = ₹60.
Total cost = ₹200 + ₹60 = ₹260.
Change = ₹500 - ₹260 = ₹240.

The change should be ₹240.


EXPECTED ANSWER:

₹240


RESULT:

PASS


========================================
TEST 2: CHAIN-OF-THOUGHT PROMPT
========================================

Prompt:

Solve the following problem. Think step by step and then give
the final answer.

A shop sells notebooks for ₹40 each and pens for ₹15 each.
Maneesh buys 5 notebooks and 4 pens. He pays with ₹500.

How much change should he receive?


RAW GEMINI OUTPUT:

First, calculate the cost of the notebooks:

5 × ₹40 = ₹200

Next, calculate the cost of the pens:

4 × ₹15 = ₹60

Now calculate the total amount spent:

₹200 + ₹60 = ₹260

Finally, calculate the change:

₹500 - ₹260 = ₹240

Therefore, Maneesh should receive ₹240 as change.


EXPECTED ANSWER:

₹240


RESULT:

PASS


========================================
COMPARISON
========================================

Direct Prompt:

The direct prompt asks the model to solve the problem and
provide the final answer. The model correctly calculated the
total cost and the change.


Chain-of-Thought Prompt:

The chain-of-thought prompt asks the model to reason through
the problem step by step before providing the final answer.
The calculation is clearly divided into smaller steps.


OBSERVATION:

Both prompts produced the correct answer of ₹240.

The direct prompt was sufficient for this simple problem.
The chain-of-thought prompt provided a more structured solution
that makes the calculation easier to follow and verify.


ACCURACY:

Direct Prompt: 1 / 1

Chain-of-Thought Prompt: 1 / 1


CONCLUSION:

For this simple multi-step mathematical problem, both prompts
produced the correct answer.

The chain-of-thought prompt made the reasoning process more
structured and easier to verify. However, because the problem
was relatively simple, there was no improvement in accuracy.
The main improvement was in the clarity and step-by-step
presentation of the solution.
