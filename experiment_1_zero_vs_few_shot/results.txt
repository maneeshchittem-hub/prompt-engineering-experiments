========================================
ANALYSIS
========================================

The zero-shot approach correctly classified 4 out of 5 reviews.
The model incorrectly classified the neutral review as negative.

The few-shot approach correctly classified all 5 reviews.
Providing three labeled examples helped the model understand
the expected classification categories more clearly.

The biggest improvement was seen in the neutral example.
The few-shot examples showed the model what a neutral review
should look like, which improved consistency.

Therefore, in this experiment, few-shot prompting performed
better than zero-shot prompting.
